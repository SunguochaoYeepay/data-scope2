查询示例：

生成的SQL查询

SELECT p.category_name, COUNT(o.id) as order_count, SUM(o.total_amount) as total_sales FROM orders o JOIN products p ON o.product_id = p.id WHERE o.created_at >= DATE_SUB(NOW(), INTERVAL 30 DAY) GROUP BY p.category_name ORDER BY total_sales DESC;
